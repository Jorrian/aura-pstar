Privacy Policy

Private by design.

Aura PSTAR does not collect personal data, create accounts, use advertising or analytics SDKs, or send study progress to a server.

Effective July 27, 2026.

Data stored on your device

Study attempts, confidence ratings, exam history, readiness status, and review queues are stored locally on the device. This information supports study, review, and readiness features and is not transmitted to Aura PSTAR servers.

Data collection and tracking

Aura PSTAR does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information through the app. It does not track users across apps or websites. The app contains no advertising, analytics SDK, account system, StoreKit product, or external checkout.

Deleting local data

Use Settings → Reset Progress to clear the app's local study history. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to the device and operating system's normal backup behaviour.

App Store distribution

If you download Aura PSTAR through the App Store, Apple may process App Store and device information under Apple's own privacy terms. Aura PSTAR does not receive payment information because the app is free and has no in-app purchases.

Support email

If you email support, the message and information you choose to include are handled through your email provider and the support mailbox. Aura PSTAR does not attach or transmit study history automatically. Do not include information that is not needed to resolve the request.

Children and students

Aura PSTAR does not knowingly collect information from any user. The same local-only design applies to all users.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's behaviour or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to jorrian@mac.com.
