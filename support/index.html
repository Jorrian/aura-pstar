Support

How can we help?

Aura PSTAR is a free iPhone and iPad study app. Support is handled by email and the app never sends your study history automatically.

Contact

Email jorrian@mac.com.

Include the Aura PSTAR version, iOS or iPadOS version, device model, and a short description of the question, explanation, or app flow that needs review. Share no information you do not want included in the email.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm that the device is running a supported operating-system version.
  • Open Settings in Aura PSTAR and note the content version.
  • For a question-bank concern, include the question number and source reference.
  • For progress concerns, remember that progress is stored only on the current device.

Progress and deletion

Aura PSTAR has no account or server copy of progress. Use Settings → Reset Progress to delete local attempts, confidence ratings, exam history, readiness status, and review queues.

Source and safety boundary

Aura PSTAR is independent and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Transport Canada. For authoritative requirements, use current official Transport Canada publications and consult your flight instructor or flight training unit.
